# Engine Evaluation

Generated by `scripts/evaluate_engine.py`. Do not edit by hand.

The catalog has no user interactions, so there is no ground truth about
which product a person would pick, and none is invented. Each scenario
declares the properties a correct answer must have; those are checked
mechanically.

## Protocol

1. Expectations below were written before the evaluator was run.
2. Run scripts/evaluate_engine.py.
3. Where the engine disagreed with an expectation, inspect it and record an adjudication in `review` WITHOUT editing the original expectation.
4. Fix genuine engine defects and re-run.

## Metric definitions

- **constraint_satisfaction** - every returned product exists in the catalog, is flagged can_be_complement, respects excluded categories and the requested gender, and is never from the anchor's own category
- **colour_compatibility_rate** - of the returned products whose colour_role is 'style', the share whose harmony against the anchor colour is >= 0.60. Products whose colour is skin_match or packaging are excluded from the denominator, because colour-matching them is meaningless
- **category_coverage** - number of distinct complement categories represented in the returned look
- **cross_category_rate** - share of returned products from a different domain than the anchor - the property that makes this a complementary rather than a similarity recommender
- **diversity** - distinct brands and the largest share held by any one brand
- **candidate_coverage** - categories that produced at least one recommendation, over categories considered
- **explanation_completeness** - share of returned products carrying at least one reason and at least one scored component
- **latency_ms** - wall-clock time for one recommend() call on a warm engine
